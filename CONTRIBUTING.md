# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, especially the kind that only come from
someone who actually printed a part and put it on a telescope.

## The most valuable thing you can send

**A print report.** If you made one of these parts, tell me what
happened — including when it worked. Fit tolerances are the weakest
part of any printed telescope accessory, because they depend on your
printer as much as on the model, and I only have one printer to test on.

Useful reports include:

- Which part, and the material
- Printer, nozzle diameter and layer height
- Whether it fitted, and if not, by roughly how much
- A photo, if the problem is visible

That is enough to tell a design error from a tolerance stack-up, and it
is the only way `D_TOPE`, `INS_D_ALOJ` and `JUEGO_EJE` ever get honest
default values.

## Design changes

Every part is generated by a script in `cad/scripts/`, so:

- **Change the script, not the mesh.** A modified STL cannot be merged
  in any meaningful sense — it has no parameters, no history and no
  diff. A three-line change to a parameter block can be reviewed.
- Keep the parameter block at the top and the console report working.
  The report is what catches a change that quietly breaks a clearance.
- If your change alters a dimension that appears in
  `docs/measurements.md` or `docs/calculations.md`, update it there too.
  Those files are the single source of truth for their numbers.

Please open an issue before a large redesign, so we don't both build the
same thing twice.

## Measurements

If you have a different tube — a 150/750, another 130 — its dimensions
are genuinely useful. The scripts are parametric precisely so they can
be retargeted, and the limiting factor is that I only have one
telescope to measure.

Send the caliper readings, not the derived values, and say where you
measured from.

## What is out of scope

**Certified solar filter material.** This repository publishes a
mechanical frame. It does not, and will not, specify or endorse any
filter film beyond pointing at commercial products from astronomy
suppliers. Please do not open pull requests proposing alternative
materials, homemade films or cost savings on this point.

## Style

- Code, comments, filenames and commit messages in **English**
- Design documentation in `docs/` is in **Spanish**
- Scripts target `rhinoscriptsyntax`, Rhino 7 and 8
- Geometry files carry the part number (`NI-04_motor_pulley.3dm`);
  scripts do not (`motor_pulley.py`) — see `cad/parts.md`
